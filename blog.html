UP Solar Subsidy 2026: 3kW Rooftop Solar Plant par ₹1,08,000 ki Subsidy Kaise Paayein?

Published: August 2026 | Verified for Uttar Pradesh DISCOMs

Agar aapka monthly electricity bill ₹3,000 se ₹6,000 ke beech aata hai, toh rooftop solar system lagwana sabse profitable investment hai. Uttar Pradesh me PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana aur UPNEDA State Subsidy ko milakar 3kW system par total ₹1,08,000 tak ki direct subsidy mil rahi hai.

💡 Key Highlight: 3kW system par Central Government se ₹78,000 aur UP State Government se ₹30,000 ki direct subsidy (DBT) aapke bank account me credit hoti hai.

2. Step-by-Step Application Process

1. National Portal Registration

pmsuryaghar.gov.in par jayein, UP DISCOM (PVVNL, DVVNL, MVVNL, PuVVNL) select karke Consumer Number enter karein.

2. Vendor Selection

Feasibility approval aane ke baad UPNEDA empanelled solar vendor choose karein.

3. Installation & Net Metering

DCR Mono PERC panels aur inverter lagne ke baad DISCOM bi-directional net meter install karega.

4. Commissioning & Subsidy Credit

Commissioning certificate ke baad 30 din me total subsidy DBT ke dwara direct bank account me aa jati hai.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ Kya subsidy ke liye specific panels lagwana zaroori hai?

Haan, PM Surya Ghar rules ke mutabiq sirf DCR-compliant (Made in India) panels par hi subsidy valid hoti hai.

❓ State subsidy (UPNEDA) ke liye alag se form bharna padega?

Nahi, National Portal par apply karne par Central aur State dono subsidies ek sath process ho jati hain.
