UP Solar Subsidy 2026: 3kW Rooftop Solar Plant par ₹1,08,000 ki Subsidy Kaise Paayein?

Published on: August 2026 | Category: UP Schemes

Agar aapka monthly electricity bill ₹3,000 se ₹6,000 ke beech aata hai, toh rooftop solar system lagwana sabse best investment hai. UP me PM Surya Ghar aur UPNEDA milakar 3kW system par total ₹1,08,000 ki subsidy direct de rahe hain.

💡 Key Highlight: Central Subsidy ₹78,000 + UP State Subsidy ₹30,000 = Total ₹1,08,000 Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) aapke bank account me.

2. Step-by-Step Application Process

1. National Portal Registration

pmsuryaghar.gov.in par jayein, UP DISCOM select karein aur consumer number enter karein.

2. Vendor Selection

Technical feasibility approval ke baad empanelled vendor select karein.

3. Installation & Net Metering

DCR panels aur inverter installation ke baad DISCOM net meter install karega.

4. Subsidy Release

Commissioning certificate ke baad 30 din me subsidy bank me credit ho jayegi.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ Kya specific solar panels lagwana jaruri hai?

Haan, subsidy sirf DCR-compliant (Made in India) panels par milti hai.

❓ State subsidy ke liye alag se apply karna hoga?

Nahi, National Portal se dono subsidies automatically link ho jati hain.
