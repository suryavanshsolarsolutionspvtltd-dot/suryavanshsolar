Rooftop Solar Plant par ₹1,08,000 ki Subsidy Kaise Paayein? (Complete Guide)

Agar aapka monthly electricity bill **₹3,000 se ₹6,000** ke beech aata hai, toh rooftop solar system lagwana sabse best aur high-return investment hai. Uttar Pradesh me **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** aur **UP State Subsidy (UPNEDA)** ko mila kar residential consumers ko 3kW system par total **₹1,08,000** tak ki direct subsidy mil rahi hai.

## 1. 3kW Solar Plant Cost & Subsidy Calculation

3kW on-grid solar system residential houses ke liye sabse popular capacity hai. Yaha pura cost aur subsidy breakdown diya gaya hai:

| System Capacity | Estimated Project Cost | Central Subsidy (PM Surya Ghar) | UP State Subsidy (UPNEDA) | Total Subsidy | Net Cost to Consumer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 kW** | ₹65,000 – ₹75,000 | ₹30,000 | ₹15,000 | **₹45,000** | ₹20,000 – ₹30,000 |
| **2 kW** | ₹1,30,000 – ₹1,45,000 | ₹60,000 | ₹30,000 | **₹90,000** | ₹40,000 – ₹55,000 |
| **3 kW** | ₹1,80,000 – ₹2,05,000 | ₹78,000 | ₹30,000 | **₹1,08,000** | ₹72,000 – ₹97,000 |

---

## 2. 3kW Solar System Lagwane Ke Fayde

* **Monthly Savings:** Har mahine lagbhag 360 se 400 units bijli generate hoti hai, jisse ₹2,800 se ₹3,500 tak ka monthly bill zero ho sakta hai.
* **Payback Period:** Pura investment 2.5 se 3 saal ke andar recover ho jata hai. Uske baad agle 22+ saal tak bijli bilkul free.
* **Warranty:** DCR Solar Panels par **25 saal ki performance warranty** aur On-Grid Inverter par **5 se 8 saal ki replacement warranty** milti hai.
